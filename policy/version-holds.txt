# Homebrew versions to skip during `just update-workspace` / `just install-workspace`, until a date.
# One hold per line: kind name version until reason
#   kind     only `brew` for now (formulae and casks)
#   version  prefix of the version to skip: `27` matches 27, 27.0.1 and 27,35
#   until    last day the hold applies (YYYY-MM-DD); afterwards the upgrade runs and the expired hold is reported
#   reason   free text, required
#
# Example:
# brew temurin 27 2026-11-15 Gradle 9.8 fails on JDK 27
